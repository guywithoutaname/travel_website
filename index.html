Heritage & Forts

Walls that remember empires

India's forts tell the story of kingdoms, sieges and craftsmanship spanning a thousand years.

  • Raigad & Sinhagad, Maharashtra — the Maratha strongholds of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, reached by misty mountain trails.
  • Pratapgad, Maharashtra — a legendary fort above the Konkan, a short drive from Mahabaleshwar.
  • Amer Fort, Jaipur — honey-coloured sandstone and mirror-lined halls overlooking Maota Lake.
  • Mehrangarh, Jodhpur — one of India's largest forts, rising over the blue city.

Tip: visit forts early morning for cooler weather and softer light.

Hill Stations & Nature

Travel Guide

Good to know before you go

  1. Best season: October to March suits most regions; the monsoon (June–September) is magical in the Sahyadris and Kerala.
  2. Getting around: trains connect almost every city — book early on IRCTC for popular routes.
  3. Dress code: carry a scarf or covered clothing for temples, mosques and gurudwaras.
  4. Stay healthy: drink sealed or filtered water and enjoy street food where it is freshly cooked.
  5. Respect local customs: remove footwear where asked and always ask before photographing people.

Learning a few words — namaste, dhanyavaad — opens hearts everywhere you go.

Explore responsibly

India's beauty depends on the travellers who visit it. Leave every fort, beach and trail cleaner than you found it.

  • Carry a reusable bottle and bag
  • Stay on marked trails in hill stations
  • Support local guides, artisans and homestays
  • Never deface heritage walls
